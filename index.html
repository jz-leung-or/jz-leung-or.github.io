01 / THE INTRODUCTION

A little about me.

BEHIND THE EQUATIONS ↗
✳

I’m fascinated by the intersection of rigorous mathematical theory and the practical decisions that shape our world.

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Management Science and Engineering at the School of Management, Beijing Institute of Technology, advised by Professor Lun Ran.

My work explores distributionally robust optimization, dynamic programming, and decision-making under uncertainty, with applications in supply chains, energy systems, electric mobility and intelligent operations.

I have also engaged in research collaborations with scholars at The Chinese University of Hong Kong and The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology.
